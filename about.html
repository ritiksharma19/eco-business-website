Who We Are

RS EcoFiber — Rooted in Nandurbar

RS EcoFiber is a banana fiber manufacturer located in Nandurbar district, Maharashtra. Our region, along with neighbouring Jalgaon, forms the Khandesh banana belt — one of the biggest banana-producing areas in India.

A banana plant fruits only once. After harvest, its pseudo-stem is cut and usually discarded or burnt. We saw this waste as an opportunity: the stem contains strong, shiny, natural fiber that can replace synthetic materials in many products.

Today we collect banana stems from farmers in and around Nandurbar, extract the fiber using modern extraction machines, and supply it as raw fiber, yarn, rope and finished eco-friendly products to manufacturers, traders, artisans and exporters.

Our Mission

Waste to Wealth

To convert banana agricultural waste into high-quality natural fiber and products, creating income for farmers and jobs in rural Maharashtra while reducing the use of plastics and synthetic fibers.

Our Vision

A Trusted Name in Natural Fiber

To become a trusted supplier of banana fiber and eco-friendly products in India and abroad, known for consistent quality, honest dealing and a positive impact on our community.
