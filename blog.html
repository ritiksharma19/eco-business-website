Learn About Banana Fiber

Basics

What Is Banana Fiber?

Banana fiber is a natural bast fiber taken from the pseudo-stem of the banana plant. It is strong, light, shiny and fully biodegradable — and has been used in Asia for centuries to make cloth, rope and paper.

Process

How Banana Fiber Is Extracted

After harvest, the stem is cut, split into sheaths and fed through a decorticator machine that scrapes away the pulp. The fiber is then washed, dried in the sun, combed and graded by length.

Applications

10 Everyday Uses of Banana Fiber

Sarees, stoles, bags, mats, ropes, handmade paper, tea bags, sanitary pads, car interiors and composite boards — banana fiber is replacing synthetic material in many industries.

Sustainability

Why Banana Fiber Is Good for the Planet

It uses farm waste instead of new crops, needs no extra water or land, and breaks down naturally. Choosing it reduces plastic use and the burning of crop residue.

Farmers

Extra Income for Banana Farmers

Every banana plant leaves behind a heavy stem after harvest. Selling these stems for fiber gives farmers additional income and saves the cost of clearing fields.

Region

Khandesh: Maharashtra's Banana Belt

Jalgaon and Nandurbar together grow a large share of Maharashtra's bananas. This makes the region ideal for a banana fiber industry with steady raw material supply.
