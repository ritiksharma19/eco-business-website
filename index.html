About RS EcoFiber

Turning Agricultural Waste into Valuable Fiber

RS EcoFiber is a banana fiber manufacturing unit based in Nandurbar, Maharashtra — part of the Khandesh region, one of India's largest banana-growing belts. After every harvest, lakhs of banana stems are cut down and wasted. We collect them from local farmers and extract fine, strong natural fiber.

Our work creates additional income for farmers, employment for rural youth and women, and a clean, renewable raw material for industries looking to go green.

  • Direct sourcing from local farms
  • Machine-extracted, sun-dried fiber
  • Consistent grading & quality
  • Bulk, custom & export orders

How We Work

From Banana Stem to Fiber

A simple, clean process with no harmful chemicals.

01

Collection

Banana pseudo-stems are collected from farms after harvest.

02

Extraction

Stems are split into sheaths and passed through fiber extraction machines.

03

Washing & Drying

Fiber is washed and sun-dried to the right moisture level.

04

Grading & Packing

Sorted by length and quality, then baled or converted into products.
